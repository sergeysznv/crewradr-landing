SMS & Text Messaging Policy

Last Updated: September 18, 2026 · Toll-Free Service: +1 (855) 710-7793

1. Program Overview & Description

CrewRadr operates an automated, transactional SMS messaging service via toll-free number +1 (855) 710-7793 designed strictly for situational awareness, vehicular safety alerts, and account security notifications.

SMS notifications are triggered only upon explicit user opt-in or critical safety events:

  • Automated Vehicular Crash Detection Alerts: Instant telemetry alerts dispatched to user-designated trusted emergency contacts when high-G deceleration or severe collisions are detected.
  • Emergency SOS & Safe Landing Check-Ins: Notifications sent when a family member or crew member activates an emergency SOS distress beacon.
  • Account Security & Authentication: Transactional one-time verification passcodes (OTP / 2FA) to secure logins and account recovery.
  • Enterprise & Customer Inquiries: Follow-up text messages responding to inbound consultations voluntarily requested via web forms.

2. Carrier Non-Sharing Clause

Strict Privacy Guarantee: No mobile information will be shared with third parties/affiliates for marketing/promotional purposes. All the above categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties.

3. Explicit & Voluntary Opt-In Mechanism

In accordance with CTIA, TCPA, and carrier guidelines:

  • Consent Is Not a Requirement for Service: Opting in to receive text messages is completely voluntary. Agreeing to receive SMS is never mandatory to create an account, purchase subscriptions, download the application, or use any core features of CrewRadr.
  • Standalone, Unchecked Checkbox: When opting in via web or app forms, consent is collected through an explicit checkbox that is unchecked by default and separate from the Terms of Service.

4. How to Opt-Out (STOP)

You may cancel the SMS service at any time. Simply reply with the keyword STOP to +1 (855) 710-7793.

After texting STOP, we will send you a single confirmation text message confirming that you have been unsubscribed. No further messages will be sent to your mobile phone number unless you explicitly re-enroll.
